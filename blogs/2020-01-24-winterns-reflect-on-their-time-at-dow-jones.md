It becomes increasingly apparent how quickly time flies when you intern at a company for three weeks. Through our program, Women in Technology and Entrepreneurship in New York (WiTNY), we were able to take advantage of this opportunity at Dow Jones during winter break. It was important for us to learn as much as possible, and we believe we made the most of our experience.
