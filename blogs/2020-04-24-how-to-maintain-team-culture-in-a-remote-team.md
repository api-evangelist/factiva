How to Maintain Team Culture with a Remote Team As remote work evolves into the new global norm — at least for those not required to return to the office — many managers must adapt to working in geographically dispersed teams. Remote work is not new; pre-pandemic remote employees made up anywhere from 5.3 percent to nearly two-thirds of the U.S. workforce.
