Dow Jones expects wildfires to cause operational and travel disruption in Europe this summer, as in recent years. Blazes will likely trigger road closures, localized evacuations, and increased pressure on emergency services, especially in rural southern Europe, with past fires causing property damage in city suburbs and power outages.
