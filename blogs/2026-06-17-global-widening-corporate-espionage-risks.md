Professionals with military or government experience face growing exposure to state espionage efforts, according to Western intelligence agencies. On 3 June, Five Eyes members issued a joint bulletin warning that China's intelligence services are using a widening range of professional networking sites and online job platforms to target individuals with access to classified or privileged information.
